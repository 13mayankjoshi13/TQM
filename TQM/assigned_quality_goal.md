## Goal

The goal is to reduce software defects and uncontrolled failures by preventing invalid conditions where possible, handling exceptions safely, testing critical modules, tracking confirmed bugs and maintaining useful error logs.

## Measurement Direction

The project will track:

- defects found;
- defects resolved;
- repeated defects;
- validation failures;
- unhandled exceptions;
- error-log coverage;
- module-test status.

Actual measurements will be populated from the running project and test evidence. No invented results will be used.
